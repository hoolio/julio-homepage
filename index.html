Julio Avalos

Co-CEO, Observable · Co-founder, Shades News

I build companies with people. From GitHub's first lawyer to running its business through the $7.5 billion Microsoft acquisition — then onto Observable and Shades News.

About

I joined GitHub in 2012 as its first general counsel, when the company had about three million users and had just raised its Series A. Over the next six years I went from top lawyer to Chief Business, Strategy & Operations Officer, overseeing legal, policy, people, partnerships, marketing, and communications. We closed the $7.5 billion acquisition by Microsoft in October 2018.

Before GitHub, I was corporate counsel at Yelp through its IPO, and before that a managing associate at Orrick, where I represented Facebook and Mark Zuckerberg in the litigation that helped establish property law frameworks for data on the internet.

More recently I've been co-leading Observable alongside its creator Mike Bostock, and co-founded Shades News, a news app for a generation that wants to see things from more than one angle.

I studied English and philosophy at the University of Florida, then law at Columbia. In 2005 I published a paper in American Imago, a psychoanalysis journal founded by Freud. The through-line is something like: how do people organize themselves, and what do they owe each other?
