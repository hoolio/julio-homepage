About

I joined GitHub in 2012 as its first general counsel — fewer than 100 people, still figuring out what it wanted to be. Over six years I built the legal, policy, and business foundations for the platform that defines how software gets made. Then ran its business through the $7.5 billion Microsoft acquisition.

Now I'm Co-CEO at Observable, building computational tools for the next era of data work alongside Mike Bostock, the creator of D3.js. Also co-founder of Shades News.
