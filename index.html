I build things with people. Before that, I was a lawyer — first at Orrick, then at Yelp through its IPO, then at GitHub, where I joined as the first general counsel and eventually ran business, strategy, and operations through the $7.5 billion Microsoft acquisition.

More recently I co-led Observable alongside Mike Bostock, and co-founded Shades News, a new kind of news app for a generation that wants to see things from more than one angle.

Before all of that, I studied English and philosophy at the University of Florida, then law at Columbia — and once published a paper in a psychoanalysis journal founded by Freud. The through-line is something like: how do people organize themselves, and what do they owe each other?
