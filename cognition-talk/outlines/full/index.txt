<p>The four outlines share spine and visuals; only depth differs.</p>
<p>The 5-min is the synthesis only — drop into Act III, land on V's white space.
The 15-min compresses Acts I and II into single-wave acknowledgments; develops III, IV, V.
The 30-min is the canonical talk; every act gets its full sentence-rhythm.
The 45-min is the keynote; adds the hot-takes pass, deeper verticals, more breathing room around the moment of translation.</p>
<p>Promote/demote slides between versions by reusing the same text. The pin-factory beat (S34–S41 in 45-min) collapses to a single graphic in 30-min and disappears in 15-min. The hot takes (S115–S120) are an additive section that only appears in 45-min and could appear as one slide in 30-min.</p>
