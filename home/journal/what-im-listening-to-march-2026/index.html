What I'm listening to this month

A short running list. New discoveries, old returns.

A running list of what has stayed on repeat in March. Not reviews. Just marks.

Nilüfer Yanya — My Method Actor. The whole record. She is writing like someone who has been paying attention for ten years and has finally stopped apologizing for it.

Mk.gee — Two Star & The Dream Police. Still. A year later. Still.

Tapir! — The Pilgrim, Their God And The King Of My Decrepit Mountain. Baroque chamber-folk out of London. Dense, theatrical, not interested in being liked.

* * *

Revisited this week:

The Bats — Couchmaster (1995). Still the most underrated jangle record from that era. New Zealand indie as an entire coherent worldview.

Arthur Russell — Calling Out of Context. Put on at 1am in Madrid. It does what it always does.

The Bats — Block of Wood (1987)
