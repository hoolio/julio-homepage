The comprehension gap

A short note on the thesis of my Observable piece — and why it matters beyond code.

I wrote a piece on the Observable blog last week about what AI is doing to code. The short version: we can generate more software than any human can read. The medium — text — is failing us.

But this applies to everything. AI is generating more legal briefs than lawyers can read. More essays than editors can read. More music than any one person will ever hear. We need new mediums. Not better generation.

The output is growing geometrically. Our capacity to absorb it is growing linearly. That gap is the whole game.

The piece argues for restoring the "write / run / inspect" loop at machine speed. Computational notebooks do this for data. I think we need equivalents everywhere.
