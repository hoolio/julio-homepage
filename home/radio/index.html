Choplogic Radio

Choplogic Radio is an independent and progressive online radio show founded in the Office Ops Arts & Community Center in Bushwick, Brooklyn and now permanently broadcasting out of SAN FRANCISCO. Chop is hosted by Julio. He plays music.
New shows were uploaded regularly from 2006 to 2012. 83 episodes. This is the archive.
