Hello, world

2026-03-29

I made business cards last year for a trip to Japan. On the back, a QR code that linked directly to my Wikipedia page. It felt wrong the moment I handed the first one out. Not because the page is inaccurate — it's fine — but because someone else's platform shouldn't be the first thing you encounter when you encounter me.

LinkedIn is worse. It has the structure of a resume but the soul of a shopping mall. It doesn't represent how I think or what I care about. It represents what LinkedIn thinks people who think about careers should look like.

So this is a compromise. A room with a few doors. Links to the places I exist online, writing when I have something to write, and a design that reflects something closer to how I actually see things — which is to say, warmly, but from a slight angle.
