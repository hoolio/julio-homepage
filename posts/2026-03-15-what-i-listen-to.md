A running list of what has stayed on repeat in March. Not reviews. Just marks.

**Nilüfer Yanya — _My Method Actor_.** The whole record. She is writing like someone who has been paying attention for ten years and has finally stopped apologizing for it.

**Mk.gee — _Two Star & The Dream Police_.** Still. A year later. Still.

**Tapir! — _The Pilgrim, Their God And The King Of My Decrepit Mountain_.** Baroque chamber-folk out of London. Dense, theatrical, not interested in being liked.

::break

Revisited this week:

**The Bats — _Couchmaster_ (1995).** Still the most underrated jangle record from that era. New Zealand indie as an entire coherent worldview.

**Arthur Russell — _Calling Out of Context_.** Put on at 1am in Madrid. It does what it always does.
