The full run of *Choplogic Radio* — an independent show broadcast from Brooklyn and San Francisco between 2006 and 2012 — is now at [archive.org/details/choplogic-radio](https://archive.org/details/choplogic-radio). 83 episodes, 200 files, 6.7 GB.

It started in October 2006 from a rooftop at the Office Ops Arts Center in pre-gentrified Bushwick, which put it among the earliest independent podcasts on the iTunes directory. It predates the word "podcast" as common usage.

Indie, post-punk, electronic, krautrock, hip-hop, experimental. A weekly hour. It moved to San Francisco in 2009 and ran through December 2012.

The early podcast era (2005–2008) is one of the least-archived periods in independent audio — most shows from that window are gone. This one isn't.
